Levetor Hub Privacy Policy

Last updated: 13 November 2026

Levetor Hub is operated by Levetor Global Solutions Limited. This policy explains how we collect, use, store and protect information when you use our mobile application and services.

1. Information We Collect

Depending on how you use the app, we may collect your name, email address, phone number, delivery address, account credentials (stored as a password hash), Google sign-in identifier, device notification token, and account information.

When you place an order, we may process order details, purchased products, delivery information, payment references and transaction status. We may also maintain referral and store-credit records.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use information to create and manage accounts, process orders, arrange deliveries, support payments, provide customer service, send relevant notifications, administer referrals and store credit, and protect the security of our services.

3. Payments

Payments may be processed through third-party payment providers, including Paystack. Payment information is handled according to the provider's own privacy and security practices. Please consult the provider for details about information it processes.

4. Service Providers and Sharing

We may use hosting, database, authentication, notification and payment service providers to operate Levetor Hub. Information may be shared with these providers only as needed to provide the services, process transactions, maintain security or meet legal obligations. We do not sell personal information.

5. Data Storage and Security

We use reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect information. No method of electronic storage or transmission is completely secure.

6. Data Retention

We retain information for as long as needed to provide our services, maintain transaction and business records, resolve disputes, prevent fraud and comply with applicable legal obligations.

7. Your Choices and Requests

You may contact us to ask about the personal information associated with your account or to request correction or deletion. Some transaction records may need to be retained where required for legitimate business or legal purposes.

8. Children's Privacy

Levetor Hub is a shopping application and is not designed specifically for children.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy when our services or legal requirements change. The latest version will be published on this page.
